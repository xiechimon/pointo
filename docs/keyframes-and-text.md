# Keyframes, transcription and the text agents read

Read this before changing `src/keyframes.rs`, `src/transcribe.rs`, `src/speech_text.rs`, `src/command_transcriber.rs`, `src/sketch.rs` (`summary.md`) or `src/render.rs` (frames and the contact sheet).

## Images and text

- **Images (`frames/*.png` and `contact-sheet.png`)**: Keep images purely visual. Each keyframe shows the screenshot, active red tldraw stroke(s) for that moment, a subtle click mark (thin translucent ring and small dot, no fill, so the clicked thing stays readable; phone swipes are a thin translucent trail), and the keyframe's number alone (no `#`, no time; `summary.md` pairs each `[#N · M:SS]` with its words) in bold black on a small white rounded square with a thin dark ring, inset 5 px from the top-left corner and dodging to top-right when top-left has ink, a click or the cursor. The chip is drawn at one size on every contact-sheet tile, about 24 px with its ring: blind reading tests found 20 px failed when the sheet was shown at half size, a bare digit was read as screenshot text, and `#6` was read as `8`. `contact-sheet.png` packs frames in time order (left→right, top→bottom) into aspect-ratio-justified rows separated by tight 6 px gutters on a dark canvas (no card borders or rounded frame masks, capped at 1600 px long edge / ~1.56 MP) with no rasterized transcript headers or caption boxes.
- **Marks in `summary.md`**: a keyframe line with speech also lists every mark on that keyframe, from `sketch::describe_mark`, in brackets before the words, `;`-separated and in drawing order: `- #1 (0:09) [circled region x 0.47–0.57, y 0.40–0.44 over button “Area”] “…”` (a click keyframe starts the bracket with `clicked`; a flag keyframe shows `⚑` alone). Without speech the line is the keyframe's own `event`, which already holds the same descriptions. Coordinates are normalised x, y from the top-left of the frame, 0–1.
- **Text (`summary.md` & Clipboard)**: Put the transcript, `[#N · M:SS]` keyframe index, and absolute file paths (`Contact sheet:`, `frames/frame-XX.png`, `Video:`, `Bundle:`) in plain text (`NSPasteboardTypeString`) without repeating the transcript multiple times, so `⌘V` pastes cleanly into any terminal (Ghostty, Codex, Claude Code) or any GUI composer.

## Transcription (`src/transcribe.rs`, `src/local_asr.rs`, `src/speech_text.rs`, `src/speech_model.rs`, `src/command_transcriber.rs`)

Pointo has one transcriber, Qwen3-ASR 0.6B Q8_0 through `transcribe.cpp` (`src/local_asr.rs`), on macOS (Metal) and Windows (CPU backends). `"transcriber"` is `"qwen"` (also a missing or unknown value) or `"command"`; the old `"apple"` and `"hex"` settings parse to `"qwen"` (`transcribe::Engine::parse`), and Settings has no engine choice, only the plain row "Qwen3-ASR（本机）" (the program's name while `command` is the engine). Apple Speech, Hex as an engine and live transcription were removed in favour of this single path; don't add another engine without a requirement beyond leaving the user a way out.

The model is a verified copy in `~/Library/Application Support/pointo/models` (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Pointo\models`; `POINTO_MODELS_DIR` overrides), else a Qwen3-ASR file Hex installed (`speech_text::support_dirs`: Hex's `hex` data folder, then the older `voice-control`; reused silently, Pointo never talks to Hex). `speech_model` downloads it at launch (hf-mirror.com first, so it works from mainland China, then huggingface.co), checks size and SHA-256, retries a failed download (1, 5, 30 minutes), and re-transcribes bundles marked `.transcript-pending` once it's ready. Its state (`checking`, `downloading`, `ready`, `failed`, with progress) is what onboarding's Microphone row and Try it, and Settings › Recording show. `transcribe` never downloads for long: without a model it returns `missing` (`downloading` or `failed`), the summary gets a `No transcript: …` line (`TranscriptData::missing`), the bundle gets `.transcript-pending`, and it is transcribed again in place, `pointo.json` and `summary.md` rewritten, when the model arrives (`pointo retranscribe <bundle>` by hand).

The language is `"language"` in `~/.config/pointo/config.json` (Settings › Recording › Language: 自动识别, 普通话 `zh-CN`, 粤语 `yue`, English `en-US`; `zh-CN` → `zh`, `en-US` → `en`); absent or `auto` means the model detects it, which handles Mandarin–English code-switching. Configs from before the rename keep `"appleLocale"`, which is read as `language` and removed the next time a language is saved (`handoff::set_language_in`). Qwen3-ASR has no word timestamps, so speech islands (`audio::speech_islands`) are joined into clips by `speech_text::plan_clips` (`ClipLimits::QWEN`: islands less than 2 s apart merge, a clip is at most 30 s; the model itself reports `max_audio_ms` 87 min, so 30 s is our quality choice, lowered by `ClipLimits::for_model` if a model reports less; at most 24 clips unless merging more would pass the cap), because Qwen closes every request with 。 and a short island would come back as a sentence of its own. Each clip is transcribed on its own and `speech_text::soften_clip_end` turns the final 。/. of a clip that clearly goes on into ，/, (the clip was cut at the cap while speech went on, it ends on a word no sentence ends with such as 然后 or `and`, or the next clip carries on in lower-case Latin; questions, exclamations and the last clip keep their stop). Its words are then spread over the clip by length (`estimated: true`, which `keyframes.rs` reads to skip spoken cues): `speech_text::spread_words` treats every CJK character as a word, ends sentences at `。！？；`, and joins with pangu spacing (`打开 Settings 页面`). Real-model checks: `local_asr::tests::runs_qwen_on_a_recording` (with `POINTO_TEST_QWEN` and `POINTO_TEST_WAV`) and `downloads_and_runs_qwen`.

`"transcriber": "command"` (`src/command_transcriber.rs`, config-only: no Settings control) runs the argv in `"transcribeCommand"` (no shell, own process group, wall-clock limit, 8 MiB stdout cap) and reads one versioned JSON transcript: `source: "command:<name>"`; any failure means Qwen3-ASR for that recording, and a missing or malformed command is logged once. Nothing the program says or is given reaches the log. It gets no language from Pointo: the user's argv carries the model's. Its keys are read on their own (`Program::load_from`), never through `handoff::Config`. `--transcriber qwen|command` on `reprocess`/`import` pins the engine for one run.

## Keyframe selection (`src/keyframes.rs`)

- Only cut keyframes on real visual or input events (`Mark` strokes drawn while holding `⇧`, flags, `Click`/`Drag` inside the capture target or phone taps/swipes, `Laser` points, or settled `SceneChange` transitions)—never cut extra keyframes on speech pauses alone over a static screen.
- The one speech exception is a spoken cue with measured word timings (only from a command that gives measured times; never Qwen3-ASR, whose times are `estimated`): "now", "here", "look", "watch" snap to a transition that starts within −0.3 s…+1.2 s of the word, else take the frame 150 ms after it. A cue keyframe is dropped unless its image differs (scene diff ≥ 0.03) from every keyframe already chosen, so speech over an unchanged screen still yields one frame. Demonstratives and "see"/"there" are weak (they rank with ordinary scene changes) because they are usually filler.
- Budget (`keyframe_budget`): 6 up to 40 s, then one more per 20 s, at most 12 (each image costs a reading model ~1–1.5k tokens). Ordinary events fill only the first 6; the rest are for marks and coverage.
- Marks are never dropped. Marks drawn on the same screen share one keyframe at the last of them, which draws all their ink (`mark.frame` names it; `markId` is the first): the screen counts as the same while the luma grid around their ink, padded by 8%, changed in under 5% of its cells. A group closes at a click or drag, past 20 s, or past 12 strokes. Groups beyond the budget merge with a neighbour: same-screen pairs with the least ink first, else the most alike screens. Flags stay on their own. No other keyframe cuts into a multi-mark group's span. Auto-crop gives a group's ink to its keyframe's crop alone (`Input::pin_ink`).
- Coverage: after marks, a stretch longer than 100 words or 35 s gets a frame from inside it when one qualifies (a click, laser, settled scene change or novel cue); the lead-in before the first keyframe is its own stretch. Speech over an unchanged screen still yields one frame.
- Text per keyframe: each keyframe owns the words from the start of the clause in progress at its anchor (action start, transition start, or cue word) until the next keyframe's start. Clauses break at clause punctuation, recognizer segment starts, or a ≥1.5 s pause. Words before the first keyframe belong to it.
- Mark shapes (`sketch::marks_of`, measured in pixels): a stroke is a circle when it ends near where it began, is longer than twice its box's long side and fills a third of its box, whose short side is over 0.42 of its long side; flatter loops, to 5:1 (a wide cell or a line of text gets a flat loop), also count when they close well within their short side and go round at most about three times, so a flat C or a scribble over text doesn't. A loop like that which is most of a stroke with a lead-in, tail or overshoot is a circle too. Either way the mark's `box_norm` is the loop alone, without the lead-in or tail (a stroke gone round more than once keeps its whole box). In strokes longer than 512 points a loop's ends are tried at every nth point only, but each stretch is still judged by the whole recorded path's length and area. A straight stroke is a line or underline. An arrow is a line with one or two short strokes near one end (the head), or one stroke whose straight shaft turns back at the tip into one or two straight barbs and whose head reaches both sides of the shaft (two barbs, or one that runs on across it); `from` is the shaft's start and `to` the tip. An underline whose end hooks back to one side, ticks, V's, zigzags, scribbles and C-shaped arcs stay plain marks.

## Experiment `agentContext` (`src/agent_context.rs`)

Off by default (see Experiments in `AGENTS.md`). With it on, `sketch::summary_markdown` hands each keyframe to `agent_context::keyframe_texts`, which recovers the keyframe's timed words from its `said` and breaks them up by what happened while they were said: marks, clicks, drags, scrolls, shortcuts, and repeats of one item within 3 s folded into `×N` (`pressed ⌘T ×3`, `clicked ×4`), ordinary windows opening and closing (layer 0, by app name; titles never leave `pointo.json`), app switches by app name, ScrawlKit app events and context changes. The text never gives raw coordinates (the images show where): a place is written only when something names it. A mark is its shape, then what it was drawn on when `Mark.on` names it, then its stroke count when more than one (`✎② 0:19 circled button “Window” (2 strokes)`, `✎ 0:05 marked`); a click is `clicked`, plus its role and label when known (`clicked button “Area”`); a point is shown only with a label. An unnamed drag shorter than 0.15 of the screen is left out; a longer one is its direction (`dragged right`), and a named one names its target, `scrubbed` for a slider, scroll bar, scrubber, timeline or progress bar (`scrubbed slider “Playback”`). Drags of one thing less than 3 s apart are one line (`dragged back and forth ×2`), and a drag the dev page already reported as a video scrub is left to that report. Speech before the first item stays on the bullet line; each mark, or run of events with no speech between them, starts an indented line followed by the words said until the next one. An item's place in the speech moves back to the start of its clause if that began within 3 s, else forward past the clause's last word if it ends within 1.2 s, so a clause isn't cut in half. Events belong to the keyframe whose stretch they fall in (a stretch starts at the keyframe's first word, first mark, own moment or, for a click keyframe, its click up to 1.5 s before); marks always belong to their own keyframe. Without word times (a segment-only transcript) the speech stays whole and the items follow it.

When a keyframe draws two or more marks they are numbered ①②③ in drawing order (`sketch::marks_on`) in the text and `pointo.json`, never on the image: frames, contact sheets and exports show only the red ink, so nothing covers the screen. Single-mark keyframes stay unnumbered. `agent_context::mark_entries` / `find_mark` look marks up by those numbers: `pointo inspect --marks [--json]` lists each mark's keyframe, number, time, shape, what it was drawn on and id (`#2 ✎② 0:09 marked (2 strokes) · m2`), and `pointo zoom --mark 2 [--frame 2]` (or `--mark m2`) zooms on that mark, centred on its time span (at least `--span`, the whole mark plus half a second either side), cropped like its keyframe, with its whole ink drawn from the moment it starts. A number on several keyframes needs `--frame`; numbers shown as ①② win over single marks.

A scene-change keyframe says `[changed: the whole screen]` when its `autocrop.changes` boxes, merged when they touch, include one covering 90%. Smaller changed regions have nothing to name them yet, so the text leaves them to the image. A keyframe with no speech or items falls back to its own description without positions (`scene changed`).

With `agentContext` on when a Mac capture starts (not the iPhone mirror, and only with Accessibility granted), `crate::ax` looks up what each click and ⇧ stroke lands on in the Accessibility tree: the element at the mouse-down point, and for a stroke the middle of its box (just above a flat stroke, since an underline sits under what it marks). A click on a button's text or icon is named by the button (up to two parents). It reads the role description, a label (title, description, value, placeholder), `AXIdentifier` or `AXDOMIdentifier`, and the file name of the window's `AXDocument`; never a window title, never the value of a secure or other input field, and every string cut to one line of 60 characters. Lookups run one at a time on the `pointo-ax` thread with a 0.1 s messaging timeout and a 250 ms budget each; lookups that hit Pointo's own windows retry on the app whose window is under the point. `finish_session` waits up to 400 ms for running lookups and puts the results on `Click.role` / `Click.label` (`“New Tab” #tab-new in main.rs`), `Drag.label` and `Stroke.on` (`button “Area”`, which `Mark.on` and the mark's description pick up). Results of an earlier capture are dropped (`Prober::begin`).

Dev pages that load `/sdk.js` add what only the page knows (`src/page.rs`, `src/sdk.js`). With `agentContext` on, `/web/register` answers `"context": true` to loopback pages, and the script then hooks in once: the element under each `pointerdown` and under ⇧ mouse moves (every 250 ms) as tag, id, two classes, a label (`aria-label`, `title`, `alt`, `data-testid`, else up to 60 characters of text; for form fields only the placeholder or name, nothing for password fields, never a typed value), and the component and source `file:line` from React's fiber (`_debugSource`, or the first project frame of `_debugStack`) or a locator attribute (`data-source-loc`, `data-inspector-relative-path`/`-line`, `data-sentry-component`/`-source-file`); `<video>`/`<audio>` seeks, plays and pauses (file name, time, duration); `console.error`, uncaught errors and unhandled rejections (message and first project frame); and fetch/XHR requests that fail or answer 400+ (method, path without query, status, duration; no bodies, headers or cookies). It batches them to `POST /web/context`, which takes loopback pages from this Mac only, token or not, and drops them when the experiment is off. The app keeps 3 minutes per tab (300 events, 16 tabs) and cuts every string again. When a capture finishes (`finish_session`, not the iPhone mirror), `page::apply` takes the window's events from tabs that were clicked, marked or focused then: a page click within 400 ms of a Mac click or drag replaces its label (`<button.tab-new> “New tab” in BrowserHeader (src/BrowserHeader.tsx:88)`), the most common element under a stroke's ⇧ moves becomes its `on`, and errors, failed requests and video playback become app events (`console error: …`, `GET /api/tabs → 404`, `video clips.mp4 scrubbed to 0:04 / 0:11`, a scrub's seeks folded into its last). The agentContext summary shows a Mac capture's app events without the `app:` prefix.

## Experiment `clipboardImage` (`src/handoff.rs`, `src/win/clipboard.rs`)

Off by default; Windows only. A finished capture (and `pointo send` to the clipboard) normally copies `summary.md`'s text alone, so pressing Ctrl+V yourself drops the contact sheet; only the Paste chip and the paste shortcut paste the image first. With it on, `copy_bundle_to_clipboard` puts `contact-sheet.png` (`PNG` and `CF_DIBV5`) and the text on the clipboard together, so one Ctrl+V attaches the image and inserts the text in a browser or Electron composer (checked in the Claude desktop app) and pastes just the text in a terminal. If the contact sheet can't be read, the text is copied alone.

To compare a summary experiment on real captures, `scripts/agent-eval.sh [--experiment NAME] [--out DIR] [bundle…]` reprocesses each bundle with and without it (Kit's own `experiments` removed from the baseline's config) and writes `before.md`, `after.md`, both contact sheets and frames per bundle, and an `index.md`, by default to `~/code/home/artifacts/pointo-context/`. Both summaries come from the baseline run's transcript. What a capture recorded live (Accessibility lookups, page events) is kept as recorded, so those only show on captures made with the experiment on.
